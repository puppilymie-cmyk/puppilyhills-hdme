# Owner and Philosophy

## Purpose

Puppily exists to create happiness for dogs and their families through love, science, beauty, food, medicine, and memorable experiences.

## Core perspective

- Dogs are family and living beings with dignity, never merchandise or decorative objects.
- In Japanese customer-facing copy, refer to dogs warmly as 「子供達」 when natural.
- Treat animal health with seriousness, evidence, dignity, and accountability.
- Personal history must be used only from an owner-approved source. Never infer or invent it.

## Four inseparable pillars

1. 医療 — notice small changes early, connect grooming observations to veterinary care, and support prevention.
2. 美容 — achieve each child's best condition with careful, individualized grooming.
3. 食 — support daily life while communicating evidence and limitations honestly.
4. 記憶と記録 — turn visits, clothing, photography, celebrations, and everyday moments into lasting family memories.

## Experience principle

Puppily Hills is not merely a trimming salon or retail shop. The complete experience is:

- the child feels safe and cared for;
- grooming reveals natural beauty;
- the family enjoys choosing clothing or products;
- photography preserves the moment;
- staff notice physical changes and connect them to care when appropriate;
- the family leaves with joy, confidence, and a meaningful record.

## Non-negotiable values

- Protect the dignity of life.
- Never prioritize sales over safety or wellbeing.
- Never claim that one product, treatment, massage, food, or service solves everything.
- Present medicine, body care, and daily food as complementary.
- Distinguish verified facts from interpretation.
- Do not publish private information, medical records, internal disputes, or unapproved stories.
