# Content Safety and Approval

## Public-by-default repository rule

This repository is public. Never store:

- customer or patient personal data;
- medical records;
- private customer names or contact information;
- staff HR information;
- passwords, tokens, API keys, or account identifiers;
- internal financial or legal disputes;
- unpublished allegations;
- unapproved photos, videos, voice samples, or case details.

Use secure private systems for those materials.

## Facts requiring current verification

Always verify before publishing:

- dates, weekdays, hours, closures, addresses, booking links;
- stock, prices, discount rates, shipping dates;
- event capacity and availability;
- employee names and roles;
- product formulation and nutrition data;
- medical staffing and equipment;
- “first,” “only,” “latest,” “best,” or other superiority claims;
- scientific conclusions and citations;
- platform rules for Instagram and synthetic media.

## Medical boundary

Content may educate but must not diagnose an individual animal. Recommend veterinary examination when appropriate. Emergency signs require immediate veterinary guidance.

## Approval levels

- LOW RISK: general philosophy, approved store atmosphere, already-public evergreen information.
- REVIEW: product education, events, prices, schedules, customer stories.
- VETERINARY REVIEW: diseases, symptoms, mechanisms, treatment, nutrition comparisons, clinical cases.
- OWNER FINAL APPROVAL: avatar speech, personal stories, loss, controversial claims, partnerships, crisis communication.

## Stop conditions

Do not publish when:

- the source cannot be identified;
- facts conflict;
- consent is unclear;
- a medical claim lacks review;
- the post could identify a private person or patient;
- a generated avatar says something Mie did not approve;
- the requested automation would bypass required approval.
