# Puppily Delica

## Brand concept

Puppily Delica treats daily food as an important part of lifelong care for very small dogs. The internal concept 「食べる医療」 expresses seriousness of design; it must never imply that food diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents disease.

## Communication themes

When supported by current approved materials, content may explain:

- needs of very small dogs;
- energy metabolism and reliable daily nutrition;
- selected protein sources and ingredients;
- nutritional design intentions;
- palatability, absorption, and balance;
- the relationship between food, medicine, body care, and observation.

## Specifications

Recipes and specifications can change. Never rely on memory alone. Before publishing ingredients, quantities, origins, strain names, manufacturing methods, nutrition values, prices, or standards, verify them against the current approved product sheet.

## Evidence rules

- Separate established knowledge, product rationale, owner experience, and individual anecdotes.
- Do not turn an individual improvement into proof of causation.
- Avoid cure, prevention, guarantee, or longevity promises.
- Do not attack competing products or customers' prior choices.
- Read scientific sources before citing them.
- Request veterinary review for medical or nutrition comparisons.

## Required philosophy

Longer educational or sales content should preserve this meaning:

No single action makes everything better. Medicine, body care, and daily food all matter. The purpose is not simply to sell a product, but to share the practices genuinely continued to protect the children's lives.

Use owner-approved exact Japanese wording when available.
