# Real Stories

## Purpose

Real stories are the emotional evidence of Puppily's philosophy. They should preserve dignity, gratitude, and the family's lived experience. They are not a substitute for clinical evidence.

## Story status

Every story must be labeled internally as one of:

- DRAFT — recollection only; not publishable.
- VERIFY — facts, timeline, photos, or permissions are incomplete.
- APPROVED — facts and use permissions are documented.
- PUBLISHED — final wording and publication location are recorded.

## Required fields

- internal story title;
- child identifier or approved public name;
- family permission;
- relevant dates;
- original photos/videos and source;
- what was directly observed;
- veterinarian-confirmed information, if medical;
- what changed and over what period;
- alternative explanations or treatment received;
- approved channels;
- final owner approval.

## Writing rules

- Tell only what is known.
- Never promise that another child will have the same outcome.
- Do not omit concurrent medical treatment, body care, dietary changes, or relevant uncertainty.
- Never shame prior caregivers, breeders, veterinarians, foods, or families without verified facts and a clear legitimate purpose.
- Avoid sensational before/after framing for illness.
- Protect privacy by default.
- Use grief and loss with exceptional care.

## Archive workflow

For each approved story, prepare possible uses for:

- Instagram feed or Reel;
- LINE message;
- staff education;
- customer explanation;
- website;
- internal AI knowledge.

A story approved for one channel is not automatically approved for all channels.
