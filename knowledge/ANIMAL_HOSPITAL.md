# Puppily Animal Hospital

## Mission

Puppily Animal Hospital exists to provide serious, compassionate medical care and to support more happy time between each child and family.

## Relationship with Puppily Hills

Groomers regularly touch and observe the body. A change noticed during grooming may be reported and connected promptly to a veterinarian. This supports early consultation while respecting the boundary between observation and diagnosis.

## Communication principles

- Medical accuracy comes before marketing impact.
- Describe equipment through its verified clinical purpose, not as spectacle.
- Do not say that equipment guarantees recovery.
- A groomer may notice and report a change, but must not diagnose.
- Encourage veterinary examination when concerning symptoms or changes are present.
- Emergencies require immediate veterinary care, not social-media advice.

## Claims requiring verification

Before publication, verify:

- equipment model and specifications;
- any claim such as 「国内初」;
- indications and limitations;
- veterinarian approval of clinical descriptions;
- current staffing, hours, booking routes, and emergency availability.

## Emotional frame

Communicate hope, responsibility, and the wish to help each child and family. Personal experiences must come from a separately approved owner source and must never be dramatized for engagement.
