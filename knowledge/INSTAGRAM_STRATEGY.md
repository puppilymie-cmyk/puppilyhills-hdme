# Instagram Strategy

## Objective

Instagram should deepen trust in the integrated Puppily world, help families understand daily care, invite them into meaningful experiences, and preserve real stories. Sales are a result of trust and relevance, not the sole purpose.

## Content pillars

1. 医療 — prevention, early noticing, hospital philosophy, understandable education.
2. 美容 — grooming quality, skin and coat care, individual attention, transformations without objectification.
3. 食 — small-dog nutrition, product philosophy, evidence, preparation, feeding experience.
4. 記憶と記録 — photography, celebrations, events, clothing, family stories.
5. Owner voice — Mie's lived philosophy, honest practice, lessons, and reasons for continuing.
6. Real stories — only with permission and factual verification.

## Recommended mix

Do not repeat the same sales pattern daily. Rotate among:

- educational;
- emotional story;
- behind the scenes;
- child-centered experience;
- product or event;
- frequently asked question;
- founder message;
- staff learning or care practice.

## Post construction

1. Hook grounded in a real concern, scene, or feeling.
2. One central message only.
3. Evidence, experience, or explanation.
4. Clear boundary: what is known, what is individual, and when veterinary care is needed.
5. Gentle next action: save, share, ask, visit, book, or consult.
6. Fact-check dates, place, availability, prices, names, and medical statements.

## Media use

- Never assume access to an Instagram account means permission to download, reuse, edit, or impersonate a person.
- Use only photos and videos the owner has supplied or explicitly approved.
- Confirm consent before using customers, staff, children, voices, clinical images, or case histories.
- Do not infer diagnoses or private traits from images.
- Maintain a source and approval record for every asset.

## Avatar content

An avatar must be an authorized representation of Mie, not an independent identity.

- Use only owner-approved face and voice training material.
- Clearly define approved topics, prohibited claims, tone, and review steps.
- Never generate an unverified personal memory or present invented experience as Mie's testimony.
- Start with human approval before publication.
- Disclose synthetic media when required by platform rules or when the context could mislead viewers.
