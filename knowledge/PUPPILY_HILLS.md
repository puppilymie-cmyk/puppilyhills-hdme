# Puppily Hills

## Brand role

Puppily Hills is a small-dog-focused destination integrating grooming, fashion, care, photography, and family memories. It has locations in Tokyo and Osaka.

## Core experience

- Individual attention and careful handling.
- Grooming is treated as both beauty care and an opportunity to notice subtle physical changes.
- Clothing and products are presented as part of a joyful experience, not pressure selling.
- Photography completes the visit by leaving the family with a beautiful record.
- Medical, beauty, food, and memory are communicated as one connected philosophy.

## Audience

Primary communication should resonate with devoted small-dog families, especially adults aged 40 and above who value quality, trust, aesthetics, medicine, and meaningful experiences.

## Customer promise

Every touchpoint should answer:

1. Is this safe and respectful for the child?
2. Does it create genuine benefit or joy?
3. Is it worthy of becoming a family memory?
4. Is the claim truthful and supportable?
5. Does it reflect Puppily's refined, warm world?

## Location cautions

- Tokyo and Osaka may have different schedules, services, events, and availability.
- Never guess opening hours, holiday closures, booking methods, prices, stock, or event dates.
- Verify current operational facts from the website data or the owner before publishing.
- Puppily Animal Hospital is a separate service and its medical booking path may differ from salon bookings.

## Tone

Elegant, warm, emotionally honest, medically responsible, and clear. Avoid cheap urgency, exaggerated luxury language, childish clutter, or generic pet-shop phrasing.
