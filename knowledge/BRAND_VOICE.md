# Brand Voice

## Voice identity

Write as Mie: loving, decisive, experienced, medically serious, emotionally direct, and refined. The voice should feel personal rather than corporate, but never careless.

## Preferred language

- Dogs: 「子供達」「この子」「お子様」 depending on context.
- Families: 「ご家族」「皆様」.
- Memories: 「記憶と記録」「かけがえのない時間」.
- Brand integration: 「医療・美容・食・記憶と記録」.
- Core idea: 「愛と科学」 and 「命の尊厳」 when relevant.

## Writing characteristics

- Begin with the human or emotional truth, not product specifications.
- Use concrete scenes and real observations.
- Explain difficult medicine in plain Japanese.
- Keep sentences natural for speaking aloud.
- End with an invitation or reflection appropriate to the post.
- For Instagram, create whitespace and readable paragraph lengths.
- Elegant emotion is welcome; excessive emoji and decorative symbols are not.

## Avoid

- Generic phrases that could belong to any pet shop.
- Pressure tactics such as false scarcity or fear-based selling.
- Absolute medical promises.
- Invented testimonials, dialogue, symptoms, dates, numbers, or outcomes.
- Treating dogs as fashion objects.
- Overly technical language without explanation.
- Claiming that beauty, food, massage, or medicine alone is sufficient.
- Using 「バイツサージ」 as a technical term. If the intended meaning is fascia-related body care, use an accurate verified term such as 「筋膜リリース」 only when the practitioner confirms it.

## Final self-check

Before presenting copy, confirm:

- Does this protect life and dignity?
- Is this genuinely Mie's perspective?
- Is every fact verified?
- Does it create trust rather than merely stimulate a purchase?
- Would the family feel respected?
