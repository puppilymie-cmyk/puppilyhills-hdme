## Mandatory behavior

- Preserve the four pillars: 医療・美容・食・記憶と記録.
- Protect the dignity and safety of every child.
- Never invent operational facts, medical facts, personal memories, testimonials, or case outcomes.
- Verify current dates, prices, schedules, links, product specifications, equipment claims, and platform rules.
- Treat this repository as public. Never add personal data, patient records, private stories, credentials, or internal disputes.
- Medical and nutritional content requires the review level defined in knowledge/CONTENT_SAFETY.md.
- Avatar content and public posting require explicit owner approval.
- Commit and push durable knowledge changes so they survive cloud sessions.
